# Pricing Rules

Standard customers receive no discount.

Silver customers receive a 5% discount.

Gold customers receive a 15% discount.

Gold orders over $1,000 receive an additional
2 percentage points of promotional discount.

Total discount may never exceed 20% of subtotal.

## Enterprise Pricing

Enterprise customers may receive negotiated discounts.

Enterprise discount values are not defined in this repository.
They are supplied by the enterprise pricing service.

Application code must not hardcode enterprise discount percentages.
